# Zomato Strategy Presentation

This folder contains the executive presentation developed from the customer research project, **Zomato: The Next Order Effect**.

The nine-slide presentation brings together customer survey findings, behavioural insights and practical business recommendations.

## Presentation Highlights

- Zomato and Swiggy platform preference
- The influence of discounts and final payable prices
- Customer switching behaviour
- Zomato customer satisfaction
- Factors that could encourage repeat orders
- Strategic recommendations and business opportunities
- KPIs to measure future improvements

**Format:** 16:9 widescreen  
**Designed using:** Canva

The presentation is based on an independent survey of 52 respondents. Findings are exploratory and should not be treated as representative of all Zomato customers.
